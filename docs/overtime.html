Chapter three

Who needs overtime

Overtime is usually discussed as a cost to the City. Looked at from the other side it is something else: a set of jobs whose base salary does not reach a livable number in New York without extra hours. These are those jobs.

Most dependent on overtime

Share of everything the title was paid that came from overtime, in the latest fiscal year. Titles with at least 200 people.

What the hours are worth

The distance between base salary and what people actually take home. For the titles at the top of this list, overtime is not a bonus, it is the difference between the salary and the rent.

This does not say overtime is voluntary. The payroll data records hours and dollars, not whether someone chose the shift or was mandated onto it. In titles where nearly everyone works overtime and the share of pay is high, mandatory scheduling is at least as likely an explanation as appetite for extra income, but this data cannot tell the two apart.
