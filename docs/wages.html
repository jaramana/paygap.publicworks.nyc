Pay against rent

A raise that trails inflation is a pay cut, and a raise that trails New York rent is a pay cut you feel on the first of the month. Here is which titles kept up and which did not, in real terms, since fiscal year 2014.

Two rent measures, on purpose. The rent line above is the Bureau of Labor Statistics index for rent of primary residence in the New York area. It tracks what sitting tenants actually pay, and it is built the same way as the inflation line, so the two are directly comparable. The dollar figures below come from Zillow, which measures asking rents on units currently listed. Zillow is more visceral and less representative: if you have not moved recently, the BLS line is closer to your life.
