The retirement cliff

Pay arguments are staffing arguments. A title where most people are past twenty years of service is a title about to lose a great deal of experience at once, and that is a different conversation from a title that cannot hold onto anyone for two years.

Closest to the cliff

Where people leave

Separation rate is the share of an agency's records marked as having ceased during the fiscal year.

This is not a resignation rate. The payroll marks people CEASED without saying why, so quits, retirements, end-of-season departures, layoffs and deaths are all mixed together. Agencies that run large seasonal programmes will sit near the top of this list every year for entirely ordinary reasons. Read it as churn, not as dissatisfaction.
